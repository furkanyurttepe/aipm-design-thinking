# Practice: Journey Map (Module 02)

> Status: Practice exercise. Every row below is an **assumption** unless marked
> otherwise. No real user evidence has been collected yet.

## Scenario

- **Person:** People who recently moved to a new city or region and who enjoy
  listening to and/or playing music.
- **Goal:** Meet new people they can talk to about music.
- **Situation:** They do not know who to talk to, how or where to approach
  people, or how to start a conversation. *(Assumption — not yet verified with
  evidence.)*
- **Potential evidence sources:** People I meet at jam sessions and music events
  I attend. *(Number of reachable newcomers still unknown.)*

## Journey Map

| # | Phase | Action | Thought | Feeling | Evidence / Assumption |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unplanned evening | Wonders what to do after work | "Am I going to stay at home again tonight?" | Loneliness | Assumption |
| 2 | General search | Browses social media, event apps, and friend-matching apps; finds nothing suitable | "Can I find something in the existing apps?" | Hope of finding someone | Assumption |
| 3 | Music-focused search | Looks for concerts, jam sessions, and similar events | "Can I join a music activity on my own?" | Hesitation about going alone | Assumption |
| 4 | Looking for a companion | Looks for someone to go to these events with | "Can I find someone?" | Hope | Assumption |
| 5 | Giving up | Gives up and stays at home | "I wish there were a way to connect with someone based on my music taste." | Disappointment | Assumption |

## Pain Point

- **Phase 4 – Looking for a companion:** The person cannot find a companion to
  go to the event with, so the journey breaks here.

## Opportunities

1. Help the person attend a music activity even without a companion.
2. Help the person find someone to talk to about the music they love.

*(These overlap but are not identical. Choose one focus during Define.)*

## Interpretation (not evidence)

- Possible loop: the person wants to go to an event to meet new people, but
  feels they need someone they already know in order to go.

## Open Questions

- Do newcomers at jam sessions actually report hesitation about attending
  alone?
- How did people I meet at jams attend for the first time — alone or with
  someone?
- Do people search for a companion before attending, or simply not attend?
- Are there existing apps that address this, which people cannot find, or do
  none exist? *(Currently an assumption.)*
